# Document 04: Product Manual — Aura Pro 64mm Flat-Burr Grinder
**Document ID:** KB-MAN-04  
**Product SKU:** BC-GR-400  
**Retail Price:** €389.00  
**Specifications:** 64mm Titanium-Coated Steel Flat Burrs | 400W Brushless DC Motor | 1200 RPM | 220–240V~ 50/60Hz

## 1. Overview & Key Features
The **Aura Pro 64mm Grinder (BC-GR-400)** is a single-dose, low-retention coffee grinder engineered for both espresso and filter brewing. It features stepless micrometric dial adjustment, a magnetic 58mm dosing cup, an anti-popcorning silicone bellows hopper (capacity: 45g), and an integrated plasma ionizer (StaticShield™) around the exit chute that reduces static chaff scatter by 90%.

## 2. Recommended Grind Setting Chart
The stepless adjustment collar is numbered from **0.0 (burr chirp / zero point)** to **10.0 (coarsest)**, with 10 micro-ticks between each whole number. Always adjust toward a **finer** setting while the motor is running (or when the grind chamber is completely empty) to prevent coffee fragments from jamming the burrs:

* **Turkish / Ibrik:** `0.5 – 0.9`
* **Espresso (58mm Standard Basket):** `1.0 – 2.5` (Start at `1.8` for medium-roast Arabica: 18g in, 36g out in 27–30 seconds)
* **Moka Pot / Brikka:** `2.6 – 3.4`
* **AeroPress:** `3.0 – 4.5`
* **Pour-Over (Hario V60 / Kalita Wave / Origami):** `5.0 – 7.0` (Start at `5.8` for a 15g single cup, `6.4` for a 30g carafe)
* **Automatic Drip Coffee Maker:** `6.5 – 7.5`
* **French Press / Cold Brew:** `8.0 – 9.5`

## 3. Thermal Overload Protection & Motor Reset
To protect the 400W brushless motor from overheating or foreign-object stalls, the Aura Pro has a duty cycle limit of **90 seconds ON, followed by 3 minutes OFF**. If the grinder stalls on an ultra-light Nordics roast or triggers thermal protection:
1. Immediately turn off the main power switch and unplug the power cord.
2. Rotate the grind dial coarser to setting **`8.0`** to relieve burr pressure.
3. Wait **5 minutes** for the motor windings to cool, then press the red **Thermal Reset Button** located on the underside of the base next to the rubber rear foot.

## 4. Cleaning & Zero-Point Calibration
Never put water or wet cloths inside the grind chamber. Every 2 weeks, unscrew the top collar counterclockwise past `10.0` to lift out the upper burr carrier, and brush away oils with the included boar-hair brush. To recalibrate the **`0.0` zero point**, turn the motor on empty, slowly rotate the collar finer until you hear a faint metallic "chirp," turn off the motor, loosen the two 2mm hex screws on the numbered indicator ring, align `0.0` with the white index dot, and retighten.
