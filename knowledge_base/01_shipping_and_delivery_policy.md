# Document 01: Shipping & Delivery Policy (BrewCraft Coffee Gear)
**Document ID:** KB-SHIP-01  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies To:** All retail orders placed on brewcraftgear.com

## 1. Warehouse Dispatch & Order Cutoff Times
All BrewCraft Coffee Gear orders ship from our central fulfillment warehouse in Leipzig, Germany. In-stock orders placed Monday through Friday before **14:00 CET** are dispatched the same business day. Orders placed after 14:00 CET, on weekends, or on German public holidays are dispatched on the next business day. Once an order is handed over to the carrier, customers receive an automated tracking email with a live parcel link.

## 2. Shipping Zones, Carriers, and Transit Times
We ship exclusively across the European Union, Switzerland, Norway, and the United Kingdom using climate-neutral parcel services:

* **Zone 1 — Domestic (Germany & Austria):** Shipped via **DHL GoGreen**. Estimated delivery is **1–2 business days**. Standard shipping costs **€4.90**, or **FREE** on orders of **€65.00 or more** (after discounts).
* **Zone 2 — EU Core (Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, France, Poland, Czechia, Denmark):** Shipped via **DPD Classic** or **DHL Parcel Connect**. Estimated delivery is **2–4 business days**. Standard shipping costs **€7.90**, or **FREE** on orders of **€95.00 or more**.
* **Zone 3 — Rest of EU (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Sweden, Finland, Ireland, Baltics, Greece, etc.):** Shipped via **DHL EuroConnect**. Estimated delivery is **4–6 business days**. Standard shipping costs **€11.90**, or **FREE** on orders of **€130.00 or more**.
* **Zone 4 — United Kingdom, Switzerland & Norway (Non-EU):** Shipped via **DHL Express International** or **Royal Mail Tracked (UK)**. Estimated delivery is **3–5 business days**. Flat shipping rate of **€16.50** (free shipping thresholds do not apply to Non-EU destinations).

## 3. UK & Non-EU Customs, Duties, and Taxes
For shipments to the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Norway, prices at checkout are displayed **excluding German VAT (19%)**.
* **UK Orders up to £135 GBP (intrinsic goods value):** BrewCraft collects 20% UK VAT directly at checkout under UK HMRC rules. No additional customs duties or courier handling fees are charged upon delivery.
* **UK Orders over £135 GBP, plus all Switzerland & Norway Orders:** Shipped on a **DAP (Delivered at Place)** basis. The recipient is responsible for paying local import VAT, customs duties (typically 2%–4.5% on coffee appliances), and the carrier's customs clearance fee (approximately £11.50 / CHF 18.00) prior to final delivery.

## 4. Delivery Restrictions
We cannot deliver to **P.O. Boxes**, military bases (APO/FPO), or Canary Islands / Channel Islands addresses. In Germany and Austria, delivery to **DHL Packstations** is supported for parcels under 60 x 35 x 35 cm, provided the customer enters their 8-digit Postnummer in the "Address Line 2" field at checkout.
