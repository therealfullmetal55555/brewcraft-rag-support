# Document 07: Payment Methods, Billing & B2B VAT Invoicing
**Document ID:** KB-PAY-07  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies To:** All checkout transactions on brewcraftgear.com

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
BrewCraft Coffee Gear processes all payments through PCI-DSS Level 1 encrypted gateways in Euros (`EUR`). We accept:
* **Credit & Debit Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, and American Express (with 3D Secure 2.0 authentication).
* **Digital Wallets:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal Express.
* **European Instant Bank Transfers:** SEPA Instant, iDEAL (Netherlands), Bancontact (Belgium), and EPS (Austria).
* **Buy Now, Pay Later:** **Klarna Pay in 3** (0% APR split into 3 interest-free installments for orders between €75 and €1,500; available to residents of Germany, Austria, Netherlands, France, and Spain).

We do **not** accept Cash on Delivery (Nachnahme), cryptocurrency, paper checks, or manual wire transfers for retail orders under €1,200.

## 2. Automated VAT Invoices
Every order receives an official German/EU tax-compliant PDF invoice automatically emailed as soon as the shipment leaves our Leipzig warehouse. For retail customers within the European Union, checkout prices include the statutory VAT rate of the destination EU member state under the **EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS)** scheme (for example, 19% in Germany, 20% in Austria/France, 21% in Netherlands/Spain).

## 3. B2B Intra-Community Reverse Charge (0% VAT) Rules
Business customers registered in an EU member state **outside of Germany** can purchase tax-free at **0% VAT** under the EU Intra-Community Reverse Charge mechanism:
1. You must enter your valid **EU VAT Identification Number** (e.g., `NL123456789B01`, `FRXX123456789`) in the **"EU Business VAT ID"** box on the checkout page **before** completing payment.
2. Our checkout validates your VAT number and company address in real time against the European Commission **VIES database** and deducts VAT immediately.
3. **7-Day Retroactive Invoice Correction Rule:** If you forget to enter your VAT ID at checkout and pay full VAT, you have strictly **7 calendar days from the order date** to email **billing@brewcraftgear.com** with your VAT ID and company registration certificate for a manual invoice reissue and VAT refund. After 7 calendar days, our monthly OSS tax filings are locked and we **cannot** retroactively remove or refund VAT under any circumstances.
4. German business customers (`DE` VAT numbers) always pay 19% German VAT at checkout and reclaim it via their regular Vorsteuer return with the Finanzamt.

## 4. Promo Codes & Gift Card Stacking
Only **one promotional discount code** (e.g., `WELCOME10`) can be applied per order, and promo codes cannot be combined with loyalty point redemptions or applied to `-FINAL` clearance items. However, **BrewCraft Gift Cards** count as a payment method and **can** be combined with a promotional discount code at checkout.
