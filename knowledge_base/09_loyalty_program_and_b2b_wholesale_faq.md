# Document 09: BrewPoints Loyalty Program & B2B Café Wholesale FAQ
**Document ID:** KB-LOY-09  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies To:** Retail Loyalty Members & Commercial B2B Partners

## 1. BrewPoints Loyalty Program (Retail Customers)
All customers who create a free account on brewcraftgear.com are automatically enrolled in **BrewPoints**. You earn points on every euro spent (excluding shipping fees and taxes):

* **Silver Bean Tier (€0 – €249 lifetime spend):** Earn **5 BrewPoints per €1 spent** (equivalent to 5% back in rewards).
* **Gold Tamper Tier (€250 – €599 lifetime spend):** Earn **8 BrewPoints per €1 spent** (8% back), plus free express shipping upgrades within Germany and Austria.
* **Roastmaster Tier (€600+ lifetime spend):** Earn **10 BrewPoints per €1 spent** (10% back), priority 48-hour warranty replacement, and early access to limited-edition grinder burrs.

### Redeeming & Expiration Rules
* **100 BrewPoints = €1.00 reward voucher.** Points can be redeemed at checkout in increments of 500 points (€5.00) on any order over €30.00.
* **Pending Period:** Points remain in **`Pending`** status for **30 days** after purchase (covering our 30-day return window) before becoming **`Available`** in your wallet.
* **Expiration:** BrewPoints expire automatically if your account has **12 consecutive months of zero purchase activity**. Points cannot be transferred between accounts or redeemed for cash.

## 2. B2B Wholesale & Specialty Café Program
We supply specialty coffee roasteries, hospitality groups, and independent retail shops across Europe with wholesale pricing on BrewCraft scales, kettles, and pour-over accessories.
* **Minimum Order Value (MOV):** The initial opening wholesale order requires a minimum spend of **€1,200.00 ex-VAT** (subsequent reorder minimum is **€500.00 ex-VAT**).
* **Wholesale Discount Tiers:** Approved B2B partners receive **28% off MSRP** on orders of €1,200–€2,999, and **35% off MSRP** on orders of €3,000+.
* **Payment Terms:** Your first **3 wholesale orders** must be prepaid via SEPA Bank Transfer or corporate credit card prior to dispatch. After 3 completed orders in good standing, EU partners may apply for **Net-15 invoice terms**.
* **Commercial Warranty Note:** As stated in Document 03 (`KB-WAR-03`), our domestic Aura Pro Grinder (`BC-GR-400`) is **not** rated for commercial café espresso bar service. However, the **NanoScale Pro (`BC-SC-105`)** and **TempFlow Kettle (`BC-KT-210`)** carry a **1-Year Commercial Hospitality Warranty** when purchased through an official B2B wholesale account.
* **How to Apply:** Submit your company registration number, EU VAT ID, and café/shop website to **wholesale@brewcraftgear.com**. Applications are reviewed by our B2B Partnership Manager within **2 business days**.
