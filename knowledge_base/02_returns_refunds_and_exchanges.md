# Document 02: Returns, Refunds & Exchanges Policy
**Document ID:** KB-RET-02  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies To:** Direct-to-consumer purchases on brewcraftgear.com

## 1. 30-Day Return Window
BrewCraft Coffee Gear offers a **30-calendar-day return window** starting from the date your parcel is marked as delivered by the carrier. This extends the standard 14-day EU statutory right of withdrawal by an additional 16 days. To qualify for a full refund, items must be in clean, undamaged condition with all original cables, calibration weights, manuals, and retail packaging included.

## 2. Used Equipment & Restocking Fee Policy
We understand you may want to inspect your gear, but coffee equipment requires strict hygiene standards:
* **Unopened or Dry-Tested Items:** If you unbox a kettle, scale, or grinder to check its build quality without grinding coffee beans or descaling with liquids, you are eligible for a **100% refund** of the item price.
* **Used Grinders & Brewers (Cleaning / Restocking Fee):** If a grinder or brewer has been used with coffee beans or liquids and is returned within the 30-day window, a **15% refurbishment and deep-cleaning fee** is deducted from the refund. If the equipment returns with heavy coffee oil buildup, water scale, or missing accessories, the deduction increases to **25%**.

## 3. Non-Returnable Items
The following products cannot be returned once opened or purchased:
1. Paper coffee filters, cloth filters, and cleaning tablets once the outer hygiene seal is broken.
2. Whole-bean specialty coffee sample packs and roasting calibration beans.
3. Digital gift cards and downloadable espresso profiling guides.
4. Clearance and open-box items whose SKU or product title ends with **`-FINAL`** (unless they arrive defective).

## 4. How to Initiate a Return (RMA Process)
Do not send parcels back without an authorized **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)** number. Unregistered returns sent directly to our Leipzig warehouse are rejected at the loading dock.
1. Visit **returns.brewcraftgear.com** and enter your Order Number (format: `#BC-XXXXX`) and purchase email address.
2. Select the item(s) and reason for return to receive a prepaid **DHL or DPD return label** and your RMA slip.
3. For standard change-of-mind returns, a flat **€6.50 return label fee** (or **€14.00 from the UK/Switzerland**) is deducted from your refund. If the item arrived damaged or defective, return shipping is **100% free**.

## 5. Refund Processing & Exchanges
Once your return parcel arrives at our Leipzig facility, our technicians inspect it within **3 business days**. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within **5–7 business days** after inspection. We do not process direct cross-product exchanges; if you want a different model or color, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
