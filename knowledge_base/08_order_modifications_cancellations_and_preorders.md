# Document 08: Order Modifications, Cancellations, Pre-Orders & Transit Damage
**Document ID:** KB-ORD-08  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies To:** All open and in-transit orders

## 1. The 45-Minute Cancellation & Modification Window
To achieve same-day dispatch, our Leipzig warehouse Warehouse Management System (WMS) locks orders for automated picking exactly **45 minutes after checkout**.
* **Within 45 Minutes of Purchase:** You can cancel your order for an instant refund, edit your shipping street address, or change grind/color variants using the **"Manage My Order"** self-service link inside your Order Confirmation email.
* **After 45 Minutes:** Once the order status changes to **`Locked for Fulfillment`** or **`Dispatched`**, neither customer support nor warehouse staff can cancel the order or edit the items. If you entered an incorrect street number after the 45-minute window, you must use the **DHL or DPD tracking portal** once dispatched to redirect the parcel to a local Pickup Parcelshop, or refuse delivery so the parcel returns to us.

## 2. Pre-Orders & Split-Shipment Policy
When high-demand equipment (such as the Aura Pro Grinder) is listed as a **Pre-Order**, the estimated dispatch batch date is shown on the product page (e.g., *"Batch 14: Ships October 15"*).
* **Full Payment at Checkout:** Pre-orders are charged in full at checkout to reserve your serial-numbered unit from the factory allocation. You may cancel any unshipped pre-order at any time prior to dispatch for a **100% full refund** without fees.
* **Mixed Carts (In-Stock + Pre-Order Items):** If you order an in-stock item (such as filters or a scale) together with a pre-order item in a single checkout, **your entire order will be held** and shipped together once the pre-order item arrives. If you want your in-stock items shipped immediately, please place **two separate orders** (or contact support to pay the additional shipping fee to split the order).

## 3. Damaged-in-Transit or Missing Item Claims (72-Hour Rule)
If your parcel arrives with visible external crushing, broken glass, or a missing accessory, you must report it within **72 hours (3 calendar days) of the carrier delivery timestamp**:
1. Keep all original outer shipping cartons and internal foam inserts.
2. Provide **4 mandatory photos** to support: (a) the outer shipping box showing the shipping label clearly, (b) the damaged interior packaging, (c) the damaged product itself, and (d) the product's serial number sticker (`SN-BC...`).
3. Once verified, we dispatch a free replacement unit within **24 hours** via express courier and handle the carrier insurance claim on your behalf. Claims submitted more than 72 hours after delivery cannot be covered under transit insurance.
