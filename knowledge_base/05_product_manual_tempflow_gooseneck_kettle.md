# Document 05: Product Manual — TempFlow PID Gooseneck Kettle
**Document ID:** KB-MAN-05  
**Product SKU:** BC-KT-210  
**Retail Price:** €149.00  
**Specifications:** 0.9L (900ml) Capacity | 1200W Heating Element | 304 Surgical-Grade Stainless Steel | PID Range: 40°C – 100°C

## 1. Controls & Operating Modes
The **TempFlow Gooseneck Kettle (BC-KT-210)** uses a proportional-integral-derivative (PID) controller to heat water to exact degree increments without overshooting:
* **Single Press Dial:** Powers the base ON/OFF and starts heating to the target temperature.
* **Rotate Dial:** Adjusts target temperature between **40°C and 100°C** (or **104°F to 212°F**).
* **Hold Mode Switch (Rear Toggle):** When toggled ON, the kettle maintains your target temperature for up to **60 minutes** before entering standby mode. If lifted from the base and returned within 90 seconds, Hold Mode resumes automatically.
* **Brew Stopwatch:** Press and hold the main dial for **2 seconds** to start a count-up pour-over timer on the OLED screen.
* **°C / °F Toggle:** Press the `+` and `-` touch icons on the base simultaneously for 3 seconds.

## 2. Recommended Brewing Temperatures
* **Dark Roast Coffee:** `86°C – 89°C`
* **Medium Roast Coffee:** `90°C – 93°C`
* **Light / Specialty Filter Roast:** `94°C – 97°C`
* **Japanese Green Tea (Sencha/Gyokuro):** `70°C – 78°C`
* **Black & Herbal Tea:** `98°C – 100°C`

## 3. High-Altitude Boiling Point Calibration
At elevations above 500 meters, water boils below 100°C. To prevent the kettle from boiling dry when set to 100°C at high altitude, run the **Altitude Calibration Mode**: fill the kettle halfway with cold water, hold both the **Main Dial** and **Hold Toggle** while plugging in the base until `CAL` flashes. The kettle will heat to a rolling boil, detect your local barometric boiling point automatically, and store it in non-volatile memory.

## 4. Error Codes & Troubleshooting
* **Error `E1` (Dry-Boil Protection):** Triggered when the kettle is switched on with less than **150ml** of water or heavy limescale insulates the NTC thermistor. Unplug the base, let the kettle cool for 15 minutes, add cold water, and descale.
* **Error `E2` (NTC Sensor Open Circuit):** Contact pins on the kettle base are wet or dirty. Wipe the gold contact ring underneath the kettle dry with a microfiber cloth.
* **Error `E3` (SSR Over-Temperature Fault):** Internal solid-state relay failure. Stop using immediately and file a warranty claim.

## 5. Descaling Instructions (Important Warning)
Descale every 2–4 weeks by dissolving **15g (1 tablespoon) of food-grade citric acid powder** in 500ml of water, heating to **90°C**, soaking for 20 minutes, and rinsing three times. **NEVER use household white vinegar**, as acetic acid degrades the internal food-grade silicone thermistor seal and leaves a permanent odor not covered under warranty.
