# Document 06: Product Manual — NanoScale Pro Espresso & Pour-Over Scale
**Document ID:** KB-MAN-06  
**Product SKU:** BC-SC-105  
**Retail Price:** €79.00  
**Specifications:** 0.1g Accuracy (0.3g – 2000g) | Dimensions: 105 x 105 x 18 mm | 1200mAh Li-Po Battery (USB-C)

## 1. Overview & Physical Design
The **NanoScale Pro (BC-SC-105)** is an ultra-compact 105mm square scale designed to fit on espresso machine drip trays while Remaining wide enough for a Hario V60 server. The hidden LED matrix display shows weight on the right (in grams or ounces), timer on the left, and live flow rate (`g/s`) across the top bar.

## 2. Operating Modes (Cycle by Double-Tapping `TARE`)
* **Mode M (Manual Mode — Default):** Both tare and timer are controlled manually. Tap `TARE` once to zero the weight; tap `TIMER` once to start/pause the stopwatch; hold `TIMER` for 2 seconds to reset to `00:00`.
* **Mode A1 (Espresso Auto-Drop Mode):** Place your cup on the platform. The scale automatically tares to `0.0g` after 2 seconds. The timer starts automatically the moment the **first drop of espresso** hits the cup (detected at `0.4g` threshold) and pauses when flow stops for 3 seconds.
* **Mode A2 (Pour-Over Auto-Brew Mode):** Place your carafe and rinsed filter on the scale. Tap `TIMER` once to arm (`RDY` flashes), then begin pouring water—the timer starts immediately upon water contact.

## 3. Battery & USB-C Charging Safety (CRITICAL)
The NanoScale Pro uses a 1200mAh lithium-polymer battery that lasts up to **28 hours of continuous active use** per charge.
* **Charger Requirements:** Charge **ONLY** with a standard **5V / 1A or 5V / 2A USB-A to USB-C adapter** using the included cable.
* **Overvoltage Lockout (`OVP` Message):** If connected to a high-wattage laptop charger or PD fast charger (>18W), the scale's protection circuit displays `OVP` and disables charging to prevent battery swelling. Unplug the fast charger and power-cycle the physical side switch (`ON/OFF`) to clear `OVP`.

## 4. Heat Protection & Calibration
Always keep the included **3mm removable silicone thermal pad** on the weighing platform when brewing above **65°C**. Direct contact between a freshly boiled server or hot portafilter and the aluminum weighing plate causes temporary thermal load-cell drift of up to `±0.8g`.
To recalibrate the load cell: turn the scale ON, tap `TARE` 5 times rapidly until `CAL` appears, tap `TARE` once more so `500.0` flashes, place the included **500g stainless calibration weight** in the center, and wait for `PASS` to display.
