# Document 03: Limited Warranty & Repair Terms
**Document ID:** KB-WAR-03  
**Last Updated:** September 2026  
**Applies To:** All BrewCraft electrical appliances and precision scales

## 1. Warranty Coverage Period
All BrewCraft electrical products come with a **2-Year EU Statutory Warranty** covering manufacturing defects in materials, electronics, and workmanship from the date of delivery. In addition, the **Aura Pro Flat-Burr Grinder (SKU: BC-GR-400)** includes an extra **1-Year Extended Motor Warranty** (total **3 years** on the brushless DC motor assembly) when registered at **brewcraftgear.com/register** within 60 days of purchase.

## 2. What Is Covered
During the warranty period, BrewCraft will, at its sole discretion, repair the defective unit using genuine OEM parts or replace it with a new or factory-recertified unit at no cost to the customer (including two-way shipping within Zones 1, 2, and 3). Covered defects include:
* PID controller or OLED display failure not caused by liquid ingress.
* Brushless motor failure, hall-sensor faults, or PCB power failures under normal household voltage (220V–240V AC, 50Hz).
* Load-cell drift exceeding ±0.5g on the NanoScale Espresso Scale during normal use.

## 3. Strict Warranty Exclusions (What Voids Coverage)
The BrewCraft Limited Warranty applies strictly to **domestic household use** and does **not** cover:
1. **Commercial or High-Volume Use:** Using any domestic BrewCraft appliance in a café, office kitchen with more than 15 employees, coffee cart, or grinding more than **25 double-espresso doses (approx. 500g of coffee) per day** voids the warranty immediately.
2. **Hard Water & Limescale Damage:** Heating elements and temperature sensors damaged by untreated water exceeding **120 ppm (mg/L) CaCO3 hardness** without regular citric-acid descaling are excluded.
3. **Foreign Objects in Grinders:** Burr chipping, shaft bending, or motor stalls caused by rocks, pebbles, green unroasted coffee beans, or flavored/caramelized beans coated in sugar syrups.
4. **Fast-Charger Overvoltage on Scales:** Charging the NanoScale (SKU: BC-SC-105) with an unregulated USB-C laptop charger or high-voltage PD adapter exceeding 5V/2A when using a non-compliant cable.
5. **Normal Wear-and-Tear Parts:** Grinding burrs (rated for 350 kg of coffee throughput), silicone portafilter mats, and rubber hopper bellows.

## 4. Warranty Claim & Repair Turnaround
To file a claim, email **warranty@brewcraftgear.com** or ask our support assistant to escalate your ticket with: (a) your order number `#BC-XXXXX`, (b) the 12-digit serial number printed on the bottom plate (starts with `SN-BC`), and (c) a short 15-second video demonstrating the fault. Authorized warranty repairs at our Leipzig service center have a guaranteed turnaround time of **5–7 business days** from receipt of the device.
